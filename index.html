常见问题

怎么新建一个日子？ 点右下角的 ＋。

设置在哪里？ 点左下角的齿轮；也可以长按右下角的 ＋ 约半秒。

换了手机，已购买的材质怎么找回？ 设置 → 关于 →「恢复购买」，用当初购买的 Apple ID。

FAQ

How do I add a day? Tap the ＋ in the lower-right corner.

Where are the settings? Tap the gear in the lower-left corner, or press and hold the ＋ for about half a second.

New iPhone — how do I get my materials back? Settings → About → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Do my days sync to other devices? Not yet. Your days are stored only on your iPhone.

よくある質問

日付を追加するには？ 右下の ＋ をタップします。
